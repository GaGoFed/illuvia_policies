## Short description

Your whole personal life in one Windows app - todos, checklists, money, subscriptions, wishlists and vehicles. Entirely offline: nothing you type ever leaves your PC.

## Description

Illuvia keeps the parts of a life that usually end up scattered across a dozen apps, a spreadsheet and a notes file - and keeps them on your own PC.

**One window, seven things**

- **Dashboard** - what today looks like: the month's money, what is due, what is left to do, and a calendar of the weeks ahead.
- **Todos** - tasks with subtasks up to three levels, priorities, tags and due dates.
- **Checklists** - reusable packing lists and templates, with containers you fill once and reuse for every trip.
- **Finances** - a proper ledger: income, expenses, transfers, accounts and, if you want them, investments with average cost and realized gains. Amounts are exact, never rounded through floating point.
- **Payments** - subscriptions, instalments and variable bills in one place, with what is due when, and what it really costs you per year.
- **Wishlists** - what you want, where to buy it, at what price, and how the shops compare.
- **Garage** - vehicles, refuelling and expenses, with real fuel consumption worked out full-to-full.

**The modules talk to each other**

Buy something from your wishlist, confirm a subscription payment, fill up the car: each of those can become a transaction in Finances, without typing the same amount twice. Turn a module off and it disappears from the app - sidebar, dashboard and all - without deleting anything.

**Offline, and it means it**

Illuvia makes no network connections. There is no account to create, no cloud to sync with, no telemetry, no ads. Your data sits in encrypted files on your PC (AES-256, with the key held by Windows for your account) and goes nowhere else. An optional PIN or password - with Windows Hello if you use it - locks the app when you step away.

**Your data stays yours**

One button writes everything to a single readable backup file you can keep wherever you like, and one button puts it back. No export format to reverse-engineer, no subscription standing between you and your own records.

**Five languages, one currency**

English, Italian, Spanish, French and Portuguese. You pick euro or dollar once, and every amount in the app speaks it.
